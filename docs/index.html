Triangles for spatial data in R

Triangles are everywhere in spatial work: drawing polygons, joining up points, interpolating, terrain, and the meshes behind spatial models. This is a friendly guide to what triangulation is, which R package to reach for, and why the choice matters.

One county, three kinds of triangulation. Start here to see why they differ.
