trianglewins

Constrained triangulation in R: the same inputs through every backend, judged by code that belongs to none of them. Backends are laridae (CGAL), cdtr (CDT), trowel (spade) and RTriangle (Triangle).

Survey and choosing a package

Quick answers by kind of problem, CRAN first, then about 35 R packages by algorithm, upstream library, input, constraints and output control, with lineage, a capability matrix, a look at Python and what this work could offer other packages.

Triangulation in context

Why triangles are everywhere, what earcut (decido) and the GEOS functions do and do not promise, what silicate and anglr tried before, and why laridae is built on CGAL, with one set of examples run through all three.

Benchmark at scale

Times, memory and mesh quality on geoBoundaries country polygons, from 23 thousand to 6.8 million vertices: constrained, area and quality refinement, point Delaunay, and edits to a live mesh.

Notes: dimension and interpolation

Coordinate versus topological dimension and what each community means by "2D", "3D" and "2.5D"; triangles on the sphere; what linear interpolation on a mesh promises and how the mesh decides the answer.

The roadmap now lives in hypertidy/meshcore.
